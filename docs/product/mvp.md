# MVP

What the current version of JobForge does.

## For the job seeker

- **Career profile:** title, skills, years of experience, location, work
  type preference, and salary range with currency and period.
- **Automatic job search:** every 15 minutes, jobs are pulled from remote
  job boards (and optionally company career pages) using the profile's
  job title.
- **Match scores:** every job gets a 0–100 score with plain-language
  reasons. Jobs the candidate can't take (wrong country, unpaid) are
  clearly marked; salary is shown but never hides a job.
- **Notifications:**
  - Instant email for matches of 89% or more, grouped per scan.
  - Daily digest at 08:00 in the user's timezone for matches of 60–88%.
  - In-app notifications with read status.
  - Four switches to control all of the above.
- **Job tracking:** save jobs, track applications through applied,
  interview, offer, rejected and withdrawn.
- **Dashboard:** match and application stats, pipeline, top matches and
  recent notifications.

## Not in the MVP

- Accounts and login (the app runs as a single development user)
- Hosting; it runs on a developer machine
- See the [roadmap](roadmap.md)
